Internet Overview

Understanding the global network that connects the world

What is the Internet?

The Internet is a worldwide network of interconnected computers, smartphones, servers and other devices. It allows devices to communicate and exchange information.

People use the Internet for websites, email, online education, social media, video streaming, online banking and many other services.

Important Features of the Internet

Global Connectivity

Connects computers and devices across the world.

Information Sharing

Allows users to access and share information quickly.

Communication

Supports email, messaging, video calls and social media.

Common Internet Protocols

  • HTTP: Used for transferring web pages.
  • HTTPS: Secure version of HTTP.
  • TCP/IP: Provides reliable communication between devices.
  • DNS: Converts domain names into IP addresses.
